Welcome to Green Hills Coffee Plantation

Green Hills Coffee Plantation is a family-run farm in the Kenyan highlands. We grow, harvest, and process high-quality coffee beans. Visitors, tourists, and coffee buyers are welcome to tour our farm and learn more about our coffee.

Plantation Activities

Pruning
Cutting old branches to keep trees healthy and improve coffee yield.
Harvesting
Hand-picking only ripe red coffee cherries for the best flavour.
Drying
Spreading beans under the sun to reduce moisture before storage.
